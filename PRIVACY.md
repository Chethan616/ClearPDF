# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 7, 2026

**Developer:** Chethan ("we", "us", "our") — contact: chethankrishna2022@gmail.com

Artefact (previously ClearPDF) is a document, notes and photo app that works entirely on your phone.

## What Artefact collects

Nothing. Artefact has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not run a server that receives your files, your notes, your recordings, your photos or anything about how you use the app.

## Network access

Artefact does not request Android's `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot reach the network. Every document (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint), note, list, voice memo and photo is opened, edited and saved on your device by the app's own engine. Text recognition (OCR) runs on your device with models bundled in the app.

## Files and permissions

Artefact reads only the files you open or the folders you choose to list, and keeps working copies in its own private storage so it can save your changes. The microphone is used only while you record a voice memo, and the camera only while you scan or take a photo. Files you share or print are handed to the app you choose through Android.

## Children

Artefact collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and its effective date, and say so in the release notes.
