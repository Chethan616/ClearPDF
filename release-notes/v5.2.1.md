# ClearPDF V5.2.1 — Liquid Glass in What's New (2.2.1)

This maintenance release adds Liquid Glass customization to the What's New screen. The V5.2 screen had missed a feature that was already available in Settings → Liquid Glass.

## What's new

- **Customize Liquid Glass:** tune blur, refraction, tint, and more, or choose a preset. The customization controls were contributed in [PR #61](https://github.com/Chethan616/ClearPDF/pull/61).

## Download

Choose the APK that matches your device. The arm64-v8a APK is recommended for most current Android phones and tablets; the universal APK works across supported ABIs and is larger.

ClearPDF processes documents on your device. See [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/Chethan616/ClearPDF/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) for network details.
