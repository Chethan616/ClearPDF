Artefact puts your PDFs, notes, to-do lists, photos and voice notes on one beautiful desk. It is free and open source, has no ads and no accounts, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

One desk for everything
* PDFs, notes, to-do lists, photos and voice notes side by side as cards
* Tick list items right on their card, play voice notes in place
* Filter by kind, pin what matters

Real PDF tools
* Read: continuous or single page, Reading Mode, Night Mode, find, bookmarks, read aloud
* Comment: highlight, underline, strike through, draw, text and voice comments
* Fill and sign: form fields, Fill & Sign, digital IDs with live validation
* Edit text and pictures, organise, crop, compress, combine, protect with a password
* Export to Word, Excel, PowerPoint or images; scan paper and recognise its text

Quick Preview
* "Open with Artefact" from a chat or a file manager shows the PDF in a floating card over that app. Pull it up for the full viewer, close it to be right back where you were.

Notes and voice
* Light Markdown notes with photos, to-do lists, voice notes recorded from the desk

Liquid glass and expressive motion, in English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese and Russian.
