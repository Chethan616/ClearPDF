# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ClearPDF is a small, welcoming open-source project. We want every contributor to feel safe, respected, and able to participate regardless of experience, background, identity, or device.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent and ask questions before making judgments.
- Share context when reporting bugs or proposing changes.
- Give credit to other contributors and upstream projects.
- Keep discussions focused on improving ClearPDF for users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or targeted hostility.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith behavior.
- Submitting code or assets without the right to redistribute them.

## Enforcement

Please report a concern privately to the maintainers through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially where possible.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
